# NEMT v4 continuous backtest: 2025-01 to 2025-04

## Scope

- Market: BTC historical 4-hour bars
- Test interval: 2025-01-01 00:00 to 2025-04-01 00:00 (Asia/Shanghai, end exclusive)
- Initial capital: 100 USDT
- Execution assumptions: 5 bps fee and 2 bps slippage per side; funding excluded; stop takes precedence if stop and target are both touched in one bar
- Position model: one account, no overlapping positions, maximum 5x leverage
- Evaluation: win rate is diagnostic only; it is not a pass/fail gate

The win rates were 33.3%-34.5%. They are intentionally not used as a hard rejection criterion. The two profitable variants won through payoff ratios near 2.4 rather than through a high hit rate.

## Decision

v4-A ranks first in this interval, narrowly ahead of v4-B. It satisfies positive return, at least 20 trades, profit factor above 1.2, payoff ratio above 1.2, positive expectancy above 0.05R, and maximum drawdown below 20%. It does not meet the aspirational Sharpe target of 1.5, so it remains research-only.

v4.3 correctly separated base entry from pyramid permission, but its frozen pre-test rollability model approved no additions. Consequently, v4.3 collapsed to the same behavior and result as v4.2. This is evidence that the current gate is too conservative or poorly calibrated for this market state; it is not evidence that uncontrolled pyramiding should be restored.

## Next experiment

Keep v4-A as the conservative fallback. For v4.3.1, preserve the no-lookahead design and change the rollability layer from a binary gate to a calibrated three-state decision:

1. deny additions when the lower confidence bound of marginal expectancy is negative;
2. allow a small probe addition when expected value is positive but confidence is weak;
3. allow risk-budget sizing only when both expected value and confidence are strong.

The thresholds must be calibrated on a training period preceding 2025-01 and then frozen before this same out-of-sample interval is rerun. Do not tune on the January-April outcomes.
