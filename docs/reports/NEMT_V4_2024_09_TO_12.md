# NEMT v4 continuous backtest: 2024-09 to 2024-12

## Scope

- Market: BTC historical 4-hour bars
- Interval: 2024-09-01 00:00 to 2025-01-01 00:00, Asia/Shanghai, end exclusive
- Initial capital: 100 USDT
- Contract model: risk-budget sizing, 3x base leverage and up to 5x for strong signals
- Execution assumptions: 5 bps fee and 2 bps slippage per side; funding excluded; stop first when stop and target occur in one bar
- Position model: one account and no overlapping positions
- Win rate is diagnostic only and is not a pass/fail gate

## Interpretation

v4-B is the clear winner in this interval. It added 19 times across 12 trades and outperformed the no-pyramid v4-C by 33.08 percentage points while keeping maximum drawdown only 1.36 percentage points higher. The ATR exit also improved both return and drawdown relative to v4-A's breakeven protection.

This period supplies strong evidence that selective pyramiding can materially improve convexity: a 43.33% win rate still produced a 2.979 payoff ratio, 2.278 profit factor and 0.724R normalized expectancy. It does not by itself prove future robustness; the result must be interpreted together with the weaker and negative intervals already tested.

v4.3 again made no additions and matched v4.2 exactly. Its binary permission gate protected capital but failed to capture a market regime in which pyramiding added substantial value.

## Decision

- Treat v4-B as the leading benchmark for this interval and v4-A as the secondary benchmark.
- Preserve the 3x base / 5x cap risk-budget contract model; do not multiply exposure mechanically.
- Add this interval to cross-period evidence instead of tuning v4.3 on it.
- Use v4.3.1 to learn a calibrated deny/probe/full-add decision while retaining v4-A/B as conservative fallback behavior.
