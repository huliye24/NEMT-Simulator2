# NEMT v4 continuous backtest: 2024-05 to 2024-12

## Scope

- Market: BTC historical 4-hour bars
- Interval: 2024-05-01 00:00 to 2025-01-01 00:00, Asia/Shanghai, end exclusive
- Initial capital: 100 USDT
- Contract model: risk-budget sizing, 3x base leverage and up to 5x for strong signals
- Execution assumptions: 5 bps fee and 2 bps slippage per side; funding excluded; stop first when stop and target occur in one bar
- Position model: one account and no overlapping positions
- No binary pass/fail or win-rate threshold is used

## Evidence

All six variants had positive net expectancy over this eight-month interval. The fixed-pyramid variants were materially stronger: v4-B exceeded v4-C by 66.30 percentage points, while maximum drawdown increased by only 0.47 percentage points. Its low 36.23% win rate was offset by a 3.273 payoff ratio.

v4-B added 38 times across 24 trades. It also improved on v4-A by using ATR trend exits: return was 14.17 percentage points higher and maximum drawdown was 1.72 percentage points lower.

v4.3 made no additions and again matched v4.2. The current binary rollability gate therefore remains too restrictive for the trend opportunities observed in this interval.

## Research interpretation

- v4-B is the leading benchmark in this interval.
- The 69-trade sample is materially more informative than the shorter three-month windows.
- Maximum drawdown remained below 20%, but funding, liquidation tiers and order-book depth are still absent.
- This interval overlaps the September-December test. It expands that evidence; it is not an independent confirmation sample.
- Future reports should describe evidence and limitations directly rather than attach a binary pass/fail label.
