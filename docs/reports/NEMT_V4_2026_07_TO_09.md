# NEMT v4 continuous backtest: 2026-07 to 2026-09

## Scope

- Market: BTC historical 4-hour bars
- Interval: 2026-07-01 00:00 to 2026-10-01 00:00, Asia/Shanghai, end exclusive
- Initial capital: 100 USDT
- Execution assumptions: 5 bps fee and 2 bps slippage per side; funding excluded; stop first when stop and target occur in one bar
- Position model: one account, no overlapping positions, maximum 5x leverage
- Win rate is diagnostic only and is not a pass/fail gate

All variants produced positive returns. The common win rate was only 23.08%, but payoff ratios ranged from 3.61 to 4.62. This is a useful example of positive expectancy without a high hit rate.

## Interpretation

v4-A and v4-B tied for first. Only one trade received two additions; that trade accounts for the difference between the fixed-pyramid variants and v4-C. The result supports selective pyramiding in this interval, but it does not establish robustness because the sample contains only 13 closed trades.

v4.1, v4.2 and v4.3 were identical and made no additions. The v4.3 permission layer again failed to distinguish a profitable roll opportunity that the fixed variants captured. Its conservative fallback limited exposure, but also surrendered most of the interval's excess return.

None of the variants passes the full research gate because trade count is below 20 and Sharpe is below 1.5. Maximum drawdown remained below 20% for every variant.

## Decision

- Keep v4-A as the conservative benchmark and fallback.
- Do not tune v4.3 thresholds on this interval.
- Add this interval to the cross-period evidence set.
- Design v4.3.1 as a calibrated three-state permission model: deny, probe, or risk-budget add.
